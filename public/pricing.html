Billing questions

How payment & renewal work.

How do I pay — is there a card option?

Yes. In Bangladesh, send the amount via bKash or Nagad "Send Money" to the number shown in your dashboard and enter the transaction ID — we verify it and activate your plan, usually within a few hours, always same day. Everywhere else, pay by card (Visa, Mastercard, and more) via Paddle — your plan activates instantly.

What happens right after I submit a transaction ID?

Your account stays on your current limits while we verify. Once confirmed, your plan goes active immediately, your container is (re)started if it was stopped, and your 30-day billing window starts from the confirmation date — not from when you submitted.

What happens when my Free plan hits 15,000 events?

We email and WhatsApp you at 80% usage so you're not surprised. At 15,000 events your container pauses until either your 30-day cycle resets (free tracking resumes automatically) or you upgrade to a paid plan. No data is deleted — it just stops collecting new events until the cycle rolls over or you upgrade.

How does renewal work?

bKash/Nagad plans run for whichever cycle you pick — 30, 90, or 180 days — from the date we confirm your payment. We send reminders before your renewal date; if it lapses, you get a short grace period with tracking still running, then the container pauses until you renew via the same bKash/Nagad flow as your first payment. Card subscriptions via Paddle are monthly and renew automatically — no action needed unless you cancel.

How do the 3-month and 6-month discounts work?

Pick a 3-month cycle and pay 10% less than three separate monthly payments, plus we set your GTM containers up for you free (usually a self-serve step). Pick 6 months and save 20%. Same bKash/Nagad flow, one payment covers the whole cycle. These multi-month cycles apply to bKash/Nagad billing only — card subscriptions via Paddle are monthly.

Can I cancel or downgrade anytime?

Yes. There's no lock-in contract — simply don't renew when your 30-day window ends, or downgrade to Free at any time and keep the 15,000 events/month tier forever.

Do you offer refunds?

Message us on WhatsApp if something's wrong with your billing — we handle it case by case, same day.
