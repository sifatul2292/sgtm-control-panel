The problem

Your browser pixel misses purchases

Shopify records every order, but the browser pixel only reports what the visitor's browser lets it send.

Ad blockers & browser privacy

Blockers and browser tracking protections can stop third-party pixels from loading or sending.

Checkout runs off-page

Shopify checkout runs in a sandboxed flow. If the pixel doesn't fire there, the purchase never reaches Meta.

Less data, weaker ads

Missing purchases and thin match data make it harder for Meta to find buyers and for you to trust ROAS.

How Tagioo fixes it

Send every sale from your own server

Tagioo runs a managed server-side Google Tag Manager container on your own subdomain, such as track.yourstore.com.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there a Shopify app?

The Tagioo Shopify app is coming soon. Today, Shopify stores connect with a Custom Pixel under Settings → Customer events, which needs no app install or theme changes.

Do I need to know Google Tag Manager?

No GTM expertise needed. Tagioo's Setup Assistant generates ready-made Web and Server GTM container files; you import them, preview and publish. You need a Google account with a Web and a Server GTM container.

Will purchases be counted twice in Meta?

No. The browser pixel and the server event share the same event_id (the order ID for purchases), which Meta uses to merge them into one event.

What happens when the Free plan reaches 15,000 events?

Tagioo emails you as you approach the limit. At 15,000 events the container pauses until your 30-day cycle resets or you upgrade. Nothing is deleted.

How does the 3-day Pro trial work?

Choose Pro and add a card at checkout. You pay $0 for 3 days; after that the plan renews at $50/month plus applicable taxes unless you cancel before the trial ends.

Can I get help with setup?

Yes. Email hello@tagioo.com with your store URL and we'll help you finish the setup.

Does it respect customer privacy settings?

The Custom Pixel runs under the Customer events permissions you choose in Shopify (for example Analytics and Marketing consent). You remain responsible for your consent configuration.
