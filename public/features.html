First-party infrastructure

Your own server.
Your own domain.

Managed sGTM hosting

A dedicated, isolated container running server-side GTM — full server access, preview mode, and debug tools. Not a shared proxy.

Custom first-party domain

Serve tracking from your own subdomain (e.g. server.mystore.com) with automatic SSL — first-party context means better cookies and fewer blocked requests.

Custom loader

Load your tag library from your own first-party path instead of the third-party CDN — keeps tracking in first-party context for more reliable delivery.

Real-time event analytics

Watch PageView, ViewItem, AddToCart, Checkout, and Purchase events land in real time, with hourly trends and conversion metrics per container.

Purchase deduplication

Bot filtering

Offline conversion upload

Cookie life extension

Safari's ITP caps JavaScript-set cookies to 7 days, fragmenting users into "new" visitors and breaking attribution. Tagioo writes the first-party identity cookie server-side with a long lifetime — so returning customers stay recognized and your conversion windows hold up.
