Questions

Frequently asked.

What does server-side GTM do?

Server-side Google Tag Manager processes events in a server container before sending them to your chosen destinations. It complements browser tracking and gives you more control over data routing. Your website setup, consent settings and destination tags still determine what is collected and delivered.

What does Tagioo manage for me?

Tagioo provides managed sGTM container hosting, tracking-domain configuration, automatic SSL and a dashboard for incoming events. Setup Assistant generates Web and Server GTM files for your selected destinations. You connect your DNS, import and publish the templates, and verify your tracking.

What is the purchase gap alert?

Tagioo tracks your daily purchase count and compares it to your 7-day rolling average. If today's purchases fall more than 50% below that average — adjusted for time of day so it's not triggered at 9am — a red banner appears in your dashboard. Use the banner as a prompt to investigate changes in traffic, sales or your tracking setup.

What are offline conversions and who should use them?

If your store takes phone orders, cash on delivery, or has customers who buy in-person, those sales never reach Meta through normal tracking. Offline conversions lets you upload those orders directly to Meta CAPI — improving your campaign optimization with all your real sales. Upload only data you are authorized to send and verify that Meta accepts the events.

Do I need to manage any servers?

No. Tagioo handles everything: container provisioning, Nginx configuration, SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt), health monitoring, and automatic container restarts. You just point your DNS and install the web GTM container — we do the rest.

How does the WooCommerce integration work?

Download and install the Tagioo WordPress plugin from Setup Assistant. It automatically pushes the full ecommerce dataLayer — page_view, view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase, and user_data fields — so you don't need GTM4WP or any manual code. Use the generated GTM templates for your selected destinations, publish both containers, and run a tracking test.

How do I pay?

Choose a paid plan and complete card checkout through Paddle. USD plans are monthly subscriptions that renew automatically until canceled. Customers in Bangladesh can also choose manual bKash or Nagad payment in BDT; that payment is reviewed before activation. The Free plan does not require a card.

Is my tracking data safe and private?

Tagioo runs separate sGTM containers on managed hosting infrastructure and uses HTTPS for tracking domains. You control the destination tags in your GTM setup. Review our Privacy Policy for how Tagioo handles account and event data; server-side tracking still needs appropriate consent and data configuration.
