Questions

Frequently asked.

What is server-side GTM and why do I need it?

Server-side Google Tag Manager runs on a server you control instead of in the visitor's browser. This means ad blockers can't intercept your tracking events, and your data reaches Meta, GA4, and Google Ads reliably. It's essential for any store spending serious money on ads.

How is Tagioo different from Stape.io or a DIY VPS?

Stape.io is a solid international sGTM host, but it's priced in USD, has no BDIX peering for Bangladesh traffic, and doesn't take bKash/Nagad. A DIY VPS means you own the container runtime, Nginx, and SSL yourself. Tagioo is BDIX-hosted for Bangladesh speed, billed in taka via bKash/Nagad, and adds purchase gap alerts, revenue tracking, offline conversions to Meta, and a GTM template generator built specifically for GA4, Meta CAPI, Google Ads, and TikTok.

What is the purchase gap alert?

Tagioo tracks your daily purchase count and compares it to your 7-day rolling average. If today's purchases fall more than 50% below that average — adjusted for time of day so it's not triggered at 9am — a red banner appears in your dashboard. This catches tracking breaks the same day they happen, not days later when you notice bad ROAS.

What are offline conversions and who should use them?

If your store takes phone orders, cash on delivery, or has customers who buy in-person, those sales never reach Meta through normal tracking. Offline conversions lets you upload those orders directly to Meta CAPI — improving your campaign optimization with all your real sales. The feature also extends your first-party cookie lifetime to recover more repeat buyers.

Do I need to manage any servers?

No. Tagioo handles everything: container provisioning, Nginx configuration, SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt), health monitoring, and automatic container restarts. You just point your DNS and install the web GTM container — we do the rest.

How does the WooCommerce integration work?

Download and install the Tagioo WordPress plugin from Setup Assistant. It automatically pushes the full ecommerce dataLayer — page_view, view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase, and user_data fields — so you don't need GTM4WP or any manual code. Combined with our ready-made GTM templates, your WooCommerce store gets full server-side tracking for Meta CAPI, GA4, Google Ads, and TikTok in about 10 minutes.

How do I pay?

We accept bKash, Nagad, bank transfer (all Bangladeshi banks), and international cards (Visa/Mastercard). After signup, contact us on WhatsApp or email to complete payment and activate your plan.

Is my tracking data safe and private?

Yes. Each customer has a completely isolated container — your events never touch another customer's infrastructure. All traffic uses TLS 1.3 encryption. We don't read, sell, or share your event data.
