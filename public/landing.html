Questions

Frequently asked.

What is server-side GTM and why do I need it?

Server-side Google Tag Manager runs on a server you control instead of in the visitor's browser. This means ad blockers can't intercept your tracking events, and your data reaches Meta, GA4, and Google Ads reliably. It's essential for any store spending serious money on ads.

How is Tagioo different from PixelFly or BonicBD?

PixelFly uses a shared proxy architecture — your events share infrastructure with other customers. Tagioo gives every customer a fully isolated Docker container with dedicated CPU and memory, just like a private server. You get full GTM server preview mode and complete data isolation.

Do I need to manage any servers?

No. Tagioo handles everything: Docker provisioning, Nginx configuration, SSL certificate (Let's Encrypt), health monitoring, and container restarts. You just point your DNS and install the web GTM container — we do the rest.

What happens after I sign up?

You get a trial dashboard immediately. Add your website details and GTM server container ID, then point your subdomain DNS to our BDIX server. Container launches automatically once DNS resolves. Payment activates your full plan limit.

How do I pay?

We accept bKash, Nagad, bank transfer (all Bangladeshi banks), and international cards (Visa/Mastercard). After signup, contact us on WhatsApp or email to complete payment and activate your plan.

Is my tracking data safe and private?

Yes. Each customer has a completely isolated container — your events never touch another customer's infrastructure. All traffic uses TLS 1.3 encryption. We don't read, sell, or share your event data.
