The specifics

Where each alternative
falls short for Bangladesh.

Stape.io

Solid platform, priced and built for elsewhere

Stape is a capable international sGTM host with genuinely good first-party loading support. But it's billed in USD (~$15+/mo, no bKash/Nagad), runs on global cloud infrastructure with no BDIX peering for Bangladesh, support is English-only, and it doesn't have a purchase gap alert or revenue dashboard built for ecommerce owners — it's built for developers managing infrastructure, not store owners checking their sales.

DIY VPS

You become the DevOps team

Renting a VPS and self-hosting sGTM means you own the container runtime, Nginx, SSL renewal, monitoring, and restarts — plus no built-in analytics, alerts, or GTM templates. Cheapest sticker price, most ongoing work, and the first outage is on you at 2am.
