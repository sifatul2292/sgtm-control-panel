# Tagioo marketing design

Screenshot-guided Tagioo system informed by the compact layout and technical storytelling of
TrackingConsulting. No third-party copy, artwork, testimonials or guarantees.
Scope: public marketing and content pages; customer app retains its own tokens.

## System
- White and pale blue-gray sections, deep navy panels, lime action buttons and forest-green links. Compact centered headings and restrained card shadows.
- Plus Jakarta Sans for body and bold headings; Instrument Serif italic reserved for the hero.
- Homepage: centered headline above split copy/technical diagram, problem cards and
  checklist, four-step setup, navy foundation cards, capabilities, audiences,
  factual Free-plan capacity, interface capture, pricing, FAQ and navy CTA.
  Content width is 1000px; supporting pages share centered introductions.
- Use actual product captures with concealed customer fields and clear captions.
  Illustrative diagrams describe architecture, never imply delivery proof.
- Shared typography and CTA styling across marketing, docs and legal pages.
- Primary action: Start free. Secondary: setup guide or existing support channel.
- No invented numbers, customers, guarantees, uptime or destination-delivery claims.
- No movement required to understand content; respect reduced-motion settings.
- Four-point spacing scale; named palette/font tokens live in marketing.css.
- Responsive widths: 320, 375, 414 and 768px, plus desktop. Tables scroll locally.
- Keep routes, analytics, signup parameters and local billing-cycle behavior.
- USD monthly pricing is the default; BDT response enables local billing cycles.

## Palette and typography
See the `.marketing-site` token block in public/assets/marketing.css. All new styles
use these tokens. Existing landing.css components remain the layout foundation.
